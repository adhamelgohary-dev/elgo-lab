One bit, two states

A bit stores either 0 or 1. Three bits can represent eight distinct patterns, from 000 to 111.

Logic

AND returns 1 only when both inputs are 1. NOT reverses a Boolean value.

A tiny algorithm

  1. Start with the numbers 3 and 5.
  2. Add them to obtain 8.
  3. Display the result.
